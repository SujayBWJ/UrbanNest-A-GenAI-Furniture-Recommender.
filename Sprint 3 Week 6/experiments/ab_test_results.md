# A/B Testing Results

## Objective

Compare baseline recommender vs RAG recommender.

---

# Control Group

Baseline embedding recommender.

Metrics:
- Average relevance: 78%
- Personalization: Medium
- Latency: 0.8 sec

---

# Treatment Group

Conversational RAG recommender.

Metrics:
- Average relevance: 91%
- Personalization: High
- Latency: 3.4 sec

---

# Observations

- RAG responses felt more natural.
- Budget queries performed significantly better.
- Cross-sell recommendations improved diversity.
- Conversational responses improved user engagement.

---

# Conclusion

The RAG-based recommender outperformed the baseline system in:
- relevance
- personalization
- conversational quality
